VPN setup

Connect in minutes
with Tailscale.

Maemuki Bridge works over any VPN. We recommend Tailscale — free, zero-config, and built on WireGuard. The FSE laptop must run Windows.

01

Both users create a Tailscale account

Go to tailscale.com and sign up with Google, Microsoft, or GitHub. The free plan supports up to 100 devices — more than enough.

tailscale up

Run on each device after installing the client to authenticate and join the network.

02

FSE shares their device with the office engineer

In the Tailscale admin console, click ⋮ on your machine → Share → enter the office engineer's email → Send invite. They accept via the email link.

↔ For bidirectional access, the office engineer shares their device back using the same steps.
03

Verify and run Maemuki Bridge

Confirm the shared device appears with tailscale status. Then launch Maemuki Bridge on the FSE laptop — the office engineer connects using the FSE's Tailscale IP.

tailscale status

Run on the office engineer's machine to confirm the FSE laptop is visible and reachable.
